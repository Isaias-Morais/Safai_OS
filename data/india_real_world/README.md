# SAFAIOS INDIA REAL-WORLD BENCHMARK DATASET

**STATUS: INDEPENDENT BENCHMARK POOL**

Target Capacity: 100–500+ Images

## GUIDELINES
1. Images must be captured in real-world Indian urban & rural environments:
   - Roadside waste dumps
   - Overflowing municipal bins
   - Mixed street garbage piles
   - Indian street markets
   - Open drains & residential corners
2. Environmental variations:
   - Low light / night shots
   - Direct bright sunlight glare
   - Heavy clutter & overlapping items
   - Partial occlusions & small objects
   - Diverse smartphone/camera sensors & angles
3. **ISOLATION REQUIREMENT:** Images in this directory must NEVER be used in model training or hyperparameter tuning. They serve as an independent real-world transferability benchmark.
